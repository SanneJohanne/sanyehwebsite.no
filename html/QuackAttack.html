QUACK ATTACK

OVERVIEW

PROJECT PERIOD: 11.03.2025 - 17.03.2025
TEAM: Solo
MADE WITH: Unity, Procreate
PLAY ON itch.io

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Quack Attack was created as a class assignment to develop a shoot 'em up prototype.

CONCEPT

A top-down multidirectional shooter where you play as a duck defending yourself against an army of frogs. The frogs chase you around the pond shooting after you, while you fight back by shooting eggs at them. Survive as long as you can and aim for the highest score.
