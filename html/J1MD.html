MADE WITH: Unity
PLAY ON itch.io

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Just One More Day was developed during the first year of my Game Design studies. Working in a team of four, we had two months to create a pixel art game in Unity from concept to finished product.

CONCEPT

Just One More Day is a top-down psychological narrative game set in a high school. What initially feels like an ordinary school day gradually becomes something way more unsettling. The player takes on the role of Mika, helping them navigate the school day, interact with other characters, and complete various tasks. While the story is linear, the player is given the illusion of choice through the freedom to explore the school, decide where to go, and choose who to talk to.
