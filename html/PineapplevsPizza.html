PINEAPPLE VS PIZZA

OVERVIEW

PROJECT PERIOD: 19.03.2025 - 25.03.2025
TEAM: Solo
MADE WITH: Unity, Procreate
PLAY ON itch.io

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Pineapple vs Pizza was created as a class assignment to develop a tower defense prototype. The game is inspired by the strategic tower defense gameplay of Plants vs. Zombies, with a pizza-themed twist.

CONCEPT

This prototype is a tower defense game where the goal is to place different pizza toppings to stop the invading pineapples from reaching the pizza.
