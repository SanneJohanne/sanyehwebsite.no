BATTLE FIELD TOUR - CAMPAIGN

OVERVIEW

PROJECT PERIOD: 13.04.2026 - 05.06.2026
TEAM: 5 People

ABOUT THE PROJECT

In collaboration with Battlefield Tours, our group developed a marketing campaign aimed at Canadians with family connections to the Second World War, particularly those whose relatives fought in the Netherlands. The goal was to raise awareness of Battlefield Tours and encourage people to learn more about their family history. A big part of the project was researching the target audience, Canadian culture and competitors to make sure our campaign was relevant to the people we wanted to reach.

CONCEPT

The campaign, Your Heritage, Before It's Gone, is about keeping family stories alive. We wanted to find ways for people to connect with their family history beyond just reading about it. The idea was to use the senses to make these stories feel more real and personal. We came up with three campaign elements: poppy seed packages used as business cards, posters with QR codes linking to audio recordings, and a series of Facebook videos focusing on hearing, feeling, tasting and seeing. Together, they give people different ways to connect with what their relatives experienced during the war.
