PLAYTEST FRIDAY

OVERVIEW

PROJECT PERIOD: 23.09.25 (one day)
TEAM: 2 People
MADE WITH: Unity, Blender
PLAY ON itch.io

ABOUT THE PROJECT

This is a prototype made for a class assignment to create a horror game with a focus on ambience and atmosphere. Our goal with this prototype was to create a game that balanced on the edge between comedy and horror. All 3D models are our own creation. This game was made by two people in less than 24 hours.

CONCEPT

The prototype is a first person uncanny game. It takes place at the fifth floor of Kristiania University. To escape, go to the elevator and interact with the control panel. A timer will appear, showing how long it will take for the elevator to open. Meanwhile, the teacher patrols the floor, searching for you. The player must avoid him and hide. The goal is to enter the elevator and close the doors before the teacher catches you.
