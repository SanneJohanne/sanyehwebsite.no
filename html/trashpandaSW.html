TRASH PANDA STREETWARS

OVERVIEW

PROJECT PERIOD: 27.08.2024 - 7.10.2024
TEAM: 3 People

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Trash Panda Streetwars is a board game developed in one month with three people during the first semester of my game design studies.

CONCEPT

Raccoons fight for dominion over a neighborhood. Collect trash and fight other raccoons to be the last one standing.
Gather Trash cards > Trade cards > Engage in battle > Use Trash- and Battle-cards to win over yout opponents.
