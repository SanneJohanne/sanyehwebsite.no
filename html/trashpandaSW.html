TRASH PANDA STREETWARS

OVERVIEW

PROJECT PERIOD: 27.08.2024 - 7.10.2024
TEAM: 3 People

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Trash Panda Streetwars is a board game developed in one month by a team of three during the first semester of my Game Design studies. As our first project, the focus was on learning the fundamentals of game design without jumping straight into Unity or programming. Instead, we got to focus on game mechanics, balancing, rules and player interactions through prototyping and playtesting.

CONCEPT

Trash Panda Streetwars is a competitive board game where players play as raccoons fighting to become king of the streets. The goal is to collect five golden crowns by exploring the board, gathering trash and battling other players. Players can use different cards to attack, defend, steal from each other and gain an advantage. It's a mix of strategy, resource collection and turn-based battles.
